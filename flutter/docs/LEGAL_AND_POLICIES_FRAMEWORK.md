## 4. Terms of Botanical Service & Purchase Conditions

### 4.1 Statutory GST Exemption on Seeds
- Sowing seeds fall under **HSN Code 1209** (*Seeds, fruit and spores, of a kind used for sowing*).
- Under Notification No. 02/2017-Central Tax (Rate), agricultural seeds for sowing are legally exempt from GST (**GST Rate: 0.00%**). No Goods and Services Tax is billed to the customer on seeds.
- Digital itemized GST tax invoices are generated upon dispatch.

### 4.2 Cash on Delivery (COD) Safety Guardrail
- Living plants are perishable biological specimens. To protect nursery seedlings from courier abandonment, transit delays, or repeated delivery refusals, **Cash on Delivery (COD) is capped at a maximum of ₹5,000**.
- Orders exceeding ₹5,000 require prepaid digital settlement via UPI, Net Banking, or Cards.
- First-time COD orders receive an automated WhatsApp confirmation prompt before dispatch.

### 4.3 Horticultural Disclaimers & Natural Variations
- **Living Flora Variations:** Living plants are unique biological organisms. Displayed photographs show mature specimens grown under optimal Pune greenhouse conditions. Differences in foliage variegation, stem branching, leaf count, and seasonal flowering status are natural and celebrated botanical characteristics.
- **Seed Viability vs Environmental Factors:** GLOROV seeds are batch-tested for **80% to 90%+ germination rates**. Germination depends upon external variables including soil temperature, sowing depth, sunlight exposure, and hydration discipline. Free personalized sowing advice is provided by our Pune nursery desk.

### 4.4 Governing Law & Jurisdiction
- These terms are governed exclusively by the substantive laws of the **Republic of India**.
- Any disputes or legal claims shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in **Pune, Maharashtra**.

## 5. Shipping, Transit & Botanical Trust Guarantee Policy

### 5.1 Pune Nursery Dispatch Protocols
1. **Monday Through Thursday Dispatches for Living Plants:**
   - Living potted plants are dispatched **exclusively Monday through Thursday**.
   - This protocol ensures living plants never remain idle in courier transit sorting hubs over the weekend.
2. **Monday Through Saturday Dispatches for Seeds:**
   - Seed parcels are dispatched 6 days a week within **24 hours** of order confirmation.
3. **24–48 Hour Nursery Conditioning:**
   - Every plant receives deep root saturation, foliar health inspection, and pest clearance at our Pune nursery prior to crating.

### 5.2 Moisture-Shield & Root Hydration Packaging
- Roots are sealed with **coco-peat moisture retention wraps** capable of retaining critical root humidity for up to **7 days**.
- Shipped in custom **ventilated, shockproof corrugated cartons** that allow foliage respiration while safeguarding stems from vertical and lateral impact.

### 5.4 The 48-Hour Live Plant Arrival Guarantee
- If a plant arrives **Dead on Arrival (DOA)**, suffers severe stem breakage, or irreversible root rot during courier transit, it is covered unconditionally under our **48-Hour Live Plant Arrival Guarantee**.
- **Remedy:** Free replacement specimen dispatched immediately from our nursery, or a 100% refund / store credit if the seasonal cultivar is out of stock.
- **Easy 4-Hour Claim Submission:**
  1. Within 48 hours of delivery, capture 2 clear photos of the damaged plant and outer courier packaging label.
  2. Send photos via WhatsApp to **+91 80870 87224** or email **support@glorov.in** with your Order ID.
  3. Our horticulturists verify and approve claims within **4 business hours**.

### 5.5 Perishable Botanical Policy (NO Physical Returns Required)
- Returning a damaged living plant through reverse courier transit results in the inevitable death of the specimen due to packaging darkness.
- **GLOROV does NOT require customers to ship damaged plants back.** Once verified via unboxing photos, the replacement or refund is processed immediately.

### 5.6 Seed Viability & Germination Shield
- All seeds are desi, open-pollinated, 100% non-GMO, and hermetically sealed in triple-layer aluminum foil packets.
- If zero germination occurs within the specified period (typically 7–14 days) despite following sowing directions, customers receive free replacement seeds and custom soil guidance.

### 5.7 Cancellation & Refund Settlement Timelines
- **Cancellations:** 100% full refund if cancelled prior to nursery packaging and Air Waybill (AWB) generation. Once handed to air couriers, orders cannot be halted and are covered under the 48-Hour Live Arrival Guarantee upon delivery.
- **Refund Settlement:**
  - **UPI / Net Banking:** 24 to 48 hours to bank account.
  - **Credit / Debit Cards:** 3 to 5 business days.
  - **Cash on Delivery (COD):** Transferred via instant UPI or NEFT.
